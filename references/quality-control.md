# Quality assurance gates

A project passes only after reviewing the actual artifacts. Mark each item `PASS`, `FIX`, `N/A`, or `NOT TESTED`; never convert `NOT TESTED` into `PASS`.

## Research and ethics

- Every factual narration line and factual visual has a claim ID.
- Each claim points to a source locator that supports the exact wording.
- Conflicting/uncertain claims are qualified or omitted.
- Religious claims passed the strict audit; no invented hadith, quotation, ruling, dialogue, event, or prophet depiction.
- Reconstruction, hypothetical, and fictional material is labeled where necessary.
- Asset licenses and attributions are recorded.

## Arabic language

- Grammar, spelling, punctuation, terminology, and names are reviewed.
- Voice pronunciation has been listened to in the exported audio.
- Tashkeel is correct where present and selective where useful.
- RTL, Arabic shaping, mixed Arabic/Latin text, and numerals render correctly.
- Subtitles match the spoken audio, including timing and punctuation.

## Visual and narrative

- The opening communicates the premise without misleading the viewer.
- The story has a clear progression and an appropriate ending.
- Storyboard/animatic was reviewed before final animation.
- Characters, costumes, props, lighting, perspective, and geography remain consistent.
- Maps, dates, numbers, diagrams, inscriptions, and historical details are accurate.
- Illustrative/generated/reconstructed visuals are not passed off as documentary evidence.
- No text is clipped; safe areas are respected; mobile readability is checked.

## Audio and export

- Voice, effects, and music are balanced; no important word is masked.
- No clipping, unintended silence, broken media, black frames, or missing assets.
- Duration, resolution, aspect ratio, frame rate, codec, and audio format match the delivery brief.
- Final export has been watched from beginning to end with sound.
- Render logs and measured audio/output data are recorded where available.

## Final report

Include actual tested items, failures fixed, remaining issues, output paths, file specs, source package, credits/cost (only if measured), and whether local engine compatibility was verified. State if any render, audio test, or visual review was not performed.
