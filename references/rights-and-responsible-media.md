# Rights, consent, and responsible media

- Use original, user-owned, public-domain, or properly licensed visuals, fonts, music, and sound effects.
- Record each asset's creator, URL/source, license, attribution text, date obtained, and allowed use.
- A public web image is not automatically free to reuse. A paid account does not automatically grant every use; read the plan and commercial-use terms.
- Do not imitate another creator's distinctive visual identity, character, script, or sequence. Keep references descriptive and transform them into an original direction.
- Avoid deceptive synthetic media: do not fabricate archival evidence, footage, documents, endorsements, or a real person's speech.
- Obtain consent for identifiable private individuals where appropriate; do not expose private information.
- Treat violence, disasters, illness, grief, and vulnerable people with restraint. Do not use graphic imagery for shock value.
- Follow the religious policy for sacred content and representations.
- Before publishing, check the current terms for the renderer, voice service, image/video services, fonts, music, stock material, and any third-party libraries used.
