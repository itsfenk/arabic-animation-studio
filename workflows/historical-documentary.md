# Workflow: historical documentary

1. Define period, geography, central question, and audience takeaway.
2. Inventory supplied sources and identify available primary evidence.
3. Build a chronology and place-name glossary with source locators.
4. Create a claim ledger; compare important claims across independent scholarship and primary records where possible.
5. Flag contested dates, casualty counts, motives, quotations, and later legends.
6. Draft a treatment that separates established events from interpretation and reconstruction.
7. Write Arabic narration; do not invent dialogue or private thoughts for historical people.
8. Create a visual bible for period-specific clothing, architecture, maps, tools, and artifacts. Mark illustrative reconstruction.
9. Storyboard and review all maps, captions, dates, labels, and visual details.
10. Deliver the video with bibliography, reconstruction notes, source/claim ledger, and open questions.
