# Workflow: educational or factual explainer

1. Define one to three measurable learning outcomes.
2. Identify the intended prior knowledge and vocabulary level.
3. Gather reliable sources, original data, diagrams, and worked calculations.
4. Verify equations, units, assumptions, and examples independently.
5. Build a progression: question → intuitive model → explanation → example/demo → recap.
6. Use diagrams and animation to clarify—not decorate or oversimplify—the concept.
7. Avoid false certainty, misleading analogies, and inaccurate scales.
8. Provide citations and a concise further-reading list.
9. Check captions, pronunciation of technical terms, and mobile legibility.
