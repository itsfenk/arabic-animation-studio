# Workflow: Arabic heritage, culture, and folklore

1. Identify the community, region, period, language/dialect, and version of the tradition.
2. Record who documented the material and when; distinguish oral tradition, archival record, published retelling, and modern adaptation.
3. Collect variants without merging them into one falsely definitive version.
4. Avoid stereotypes, exoticizing descriptions, or claims that a practice represents all Arabs or all members of a community.
5. Preserve culturally meaningful terms and explain them in clear MSA.
6. Mark creative adaptation and invented characters/dialogue clearly.
7. Use period- and region-appropriate visuals only when evidence supports them; otherwise make the visuals explicitly illustrative.
8. Credit collectors, performers, archives, translators, and communities where appropriate.
