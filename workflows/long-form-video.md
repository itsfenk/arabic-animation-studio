# Workflow: long-form video

1. Define the central question, chapter structure, and expected viewing experience.
2. Build a research dossier and claim ledger before scripting.
3. Create a chapter outline with a distinct purpose and transition for each chapter.
4. Plan a mix of narration, interviews/quotes where licensed and verified, maps, diagrams, animated scenes, archival assets, and pauses.
5. Write in sections; track sources at claim level and keep a pronunciation/glossary sheet.
6. Create a complete animatic or chapter-level storyboard and check overall pacing.
7. Produce chapter previews and verify consistency before scaling to the full duration.
8. Maintain versioned assets, audio stems, subtitles, and a review log.
9. Deliver chapters, captions, source notes, and final export specs where useful.
