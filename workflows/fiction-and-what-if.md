# Workflow: fiction and What If

1. Decide whether the premise is entirely fictional or anchored in real-world science/history.
2. Write the rules of the fictional world and keep them consistent.
3. Separate real facts from assumptions, speculation, and invented events.
4. For science-led What If stories, verify scientific constraints and show simplified assumptions.
5. Create original characters, settings, dialogue, and visual identity; do not imitate a known creator or franchise.
6. Choose a narrative structure that fits the duration; the upstream seven-beat What If formula is an optional technique only.
7. Keep the final reveal fair: do not present a speculative outcome as a prediction or proven fact.
8. Label fictional framing clearly when confusion with real events is possible.
