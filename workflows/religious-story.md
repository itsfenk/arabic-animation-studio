# Workflow: religious video or story

1. Set content to `RELIGIOUS_STRICT` before research begins.
2. Clarify the exact subject and whether the user supplied mandatory sources.
3. Build a religious source register and claim audit first; use `references/religious-content-policy.md` line by line.
4. Separate Qur'anic text, hadith, tafsir, scholarly opinion, historical report, and modern commentary.
5. Verify exact Arabic wording, reference locator, hadith grading attribution, and any scholarly disagreement.
6. Exclude unsupported details. Never invent dialogue, unseen motives, chronology, or connective events. Never depict a prophet or use a voice to represent one.
7. Draft a narrator-led treatment and script with claim IDs attached to every religious statement.
8. Design visuals around environment, objects, geography, calligraphy from verified text, timelines, maps, and abstract explanatory graphics—not prophet depictions.
9. Run the religious audit after script lock and again after final edit; ensure no visual cue adds an unsupported claim.
10. Deliver the source audit with the video. If a central issue remains unresolved, stop and ask rather than publish it as fact.
