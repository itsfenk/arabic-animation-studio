# Workflow: short-form video

1. Identify the one idea the viewer should remember.
2. Hook with a truthful question, striking image, contrast, or demonstration.
3. Remove context that is not essential, but do not remove qualifiers needed for accuracy.
4. Keep narration concise and visual; allow enough screen time for reading Arabic captions and numbers.
5. Design vertical or square framing only after confirming platform requirements.
6. Use fast pacing selectively; avoid rapid cuts that make diagrams or evidence unreadable.
7. End with a clear takeaway or honest open question, not a misleading cliffhanger.
8. Review on a phone-sized preview with sound and captions.
